OUR COMPANY

IngredientCore.
Focused on food ingredients.

Founded in 2025, IngredientCore is a food ingredient export company and a subsidiary of Bespring Chemical.

We work with overseas food manufacturers and ingredient distributors on the sourcing and export discussion for phosphates, propionates, sorbates, sodium CMC and hydrocolloids.

For each enquiry, tell us the ingredient or function, food application, target specification, destination market and document requirements. We use these details to review the proposed product, source and export terms with you.
