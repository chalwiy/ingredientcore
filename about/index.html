Meet IngredientCore

IngredientCore is the English brand of 鸿源配料有限公司, a food ingredient export company and a subsidiary of Bespring Chemical.

Established in 2025 and based in Xuzhou, Jiangsu, China, we focus on B2B food ingredient exports for global markets. Our customers include food manufacturers, ingredient importers and distributors.

Our role is to discuss your product requirements, proposed grades and sources, supporting records and commercial terms. The producer and manufacturing site are identified for the specific product offer.

IngredientCore and
Bespring Chemical

IngredientCore’s food ingredient export business was established in 2025. Bespring Chemical is our parent company, with an earlier phosphate business background described in its own company profile.

According to that profile, Bespring’s roots reach back to the 1970s and the former Pizhou No. 2 Chemical Plant; it was restructured under its current name in 2014.

These milestones describe the parent’s background. IngredientCore’s business is presented separately, with the producer, source and applicable records reviewed for each proposed supply.
