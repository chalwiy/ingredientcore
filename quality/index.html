SPEC

Product specification

The proposed grade’s parameters, limits or ranges, units and test methods.

Request the current version, including test conditions, reporting basis and hydration form where relevant.

Request SPEC ↗
TDS

Technical Data Sheet

Technical background, product characteristics and guidance relevant to the grade.

Distinguish typical properties and application guidance from the agreed supply limits.

Request TDS ↗
COA

Certificate of Analysis

Reported analytical results for the identified sample or production batch.

State whether you need a sample COA, a representative COA or records for a specific supplied batch.

Request COA ↗
SDS

Safety Data Sheet

Hazard communication, handling, storage and emergency information.

Specify the product and required language or jurisdiction. An SDS does not replace food-use documentation.

Quick purchasing answers

Can I download all records from this page?

This page explains the documents you can request. Contact sales for the relevant product and source-specific versions; there is no public document download library here.

Does a COA replace a specification?

No. The specification defines the proposed grade’s limits and methods. A COA reports results for an identified sample or batch.

How do I arrange a free sample?

Name the product or grade, trial purpose and destination. Sales will discuss quantity and shipping arrangements. Free samples do not imply free freight or a fixed dispatch time.
