Privacy & inquiry data.

What happens when you browse the site or prepare an ingredient inquiry.

Last revised: 3 October 2026.

IngredientCore is the food ingredient export brand of 鸿源配料有限公司, a subsidiary of Bespring Chemical. This notice explains browsing this static website and using its inquiry tools.

You prepare the inquiry; you send it.

The form creates an email draft or text to copy. It does not submit your inquiry to a website inbox. Browsing, copying and using an email service involve separate data handling, explained below.

  • Information you choose to enter
  • Product and application context in links
  • Preparing an email draft
  • Copying the inquiry
  • Cookies, browser storage and page resources
  • Hosting and technical access information
  • Email, phone, WhatsApp and external links
  • Privacy questions and notice updates

Information you choose to enter

The inquiry form asks for your name, business email and product or target function. You choose a request type: product or quotation, sample, documents, or application and selection. Optional fields include company, destination market, food application, target function, quantity and timing, specification, documents or certificates, review or trial purpose, and other questions.

Provide only the information needed to discuss your request. Avoid full confidential formulas, passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information. There is no file-upload feature; attachments can be added in your own email application when appropriate.

Product and application context in links

Links to the Contact page may include a product, application, goal, document topic, purpose or reference-page key in the URL. The page uses this context to prefill the inquiry, and includes the recognized reference page in the draft.

Page URLs can appear in browser history, shared links and hosting records. Keep this context non-sensitive: do not place personal details or confidential formulation information in a URL. Information you type into the form is not added to the browsing URL by our scripts.

Preparing an email draft

The form processes your entries in the browser to check required fields and create inquiry text. It has no online submission endpoint and does not send the form contents to our website server.

When you choose “Open email draft,” the page displays the complete inquiry and passes the recipient, subject and message to your configured email application through a mailto link. Review the draft and send it yourself. The email application or webmail service may save or synchronize the draft before you send it, according to its settings.

If the text is too long for a reliable email link, the page displays it for manual copying instead. Opening a draft does not confirm that an email has been sent or received.

Copying the inquiry

When you choose “Copy inquiry for webmail,” the page attempts to write the complete inquiry, including the recipient and subject, to your clipboard using your browser’s Clipboard API. The browser may allow the action, ask for permission or block it, depending on its security rules and settings. The page also shows selectable text. If automatic copying is unavailable, use your device’s Copy command and paste the text into your email application.

Copying does not submit the inquiry. Your operating system may retain clipboard history or synchronize clipboard contents between devices, depending on your settings. The website does not read your existing clipboard contents.

Cookies, browser storage and page resources

The current website scripts do not set cookies or use localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB to retain inquiries. They do not register a service worker or send analytics events. Page styles, scripts and images are served from the same website; fonts use the device’s installed fonts. No third-party font service, embedded widget, analytics tool or external form service is included in the current pages.

The website has no draft-saving feature. Your browser may still cache page resources, autofill fields or restore entered text when a tab or session is restored. These browser features are separate from our inquiry tool. Without JavaScript, you can read the site and use the Contact page’s email template or direct contact links; the draft and copy buttons are unavailable.

Hosting and technical access information

Serving a page involves requests to the hosting infrastructure, which receives technical information such as an IP address and the requested URL. Hosting and security services may keep access records even though the inquiry form does not submit your entries.

If this site is served through GitHub Pages, GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection (new tab). This notice does not specify a hosting-log retention period.

Email, phone, WhatsApp and external links

When you send an inquiry to us, IngredientCore receives the information you include. Your email provider and the recipient’s email services also handle the message. The website’s lack of a submission endpoint does not mean that correspondence sent to the company is never collected. Contacting us by phone or WhatsApp also involves your chosen communications provider. This website does not automatically send an email or WhatsApp message, and cannot confirm message delivery.

WhatsApp links and external technical references open the relevant outside service when you follow them. Those services have their own privacy practices. Our pages request that the browser omit the referring page address when navigating away; the outside service still receives the information needed to handle your visit.

Privacy questions and notice updates

For a question about this website, an inquiry you sent, or a request concerning your personal information, email ingredientcore@bespringchem.com. Include enough context to identify the website visit or correspondence, without sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Contact address: 10th Floor, Building 6, Greenland Linghai, Yunlong District, Xuzhou, Jiangsu 221000, China. You can also view our contact options and inquiry instructions.

If you need information about how a sent inquiry is handled, including who can access it or how long it is retained, use the privacy contact above before sharing information that requires specific handling.

This notice describes the current website tools. The revision date will change when this explanation is revised.
