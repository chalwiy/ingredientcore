Privacy & inquiry data.

What happens when you browse the site or prepare an ingredient inquiry.

Information you choose to enter

The Contact page asks for a name, reply email and ingredient or target function. You may also add a company, food application, destination market, target specification, quantity, document request and other questions. Enter only what is needed for the inquiry; avoid sending a confidential full formula in an initial message.

Links to Contact may carry a product, application, goal, document topic or reference-page key in the URL. These are intended as non-sensitive context. URLs can be visible to your browser, the hosting service and anyone with whom you share a link. Do not put personal details or confidential formulation information in a URL.

Drafts, copying and email

This website has no online form submission endpoint. While you prepare a brief, entries remain in the open browser page. “Open email draft” passes the text to your chosen mail application through a mailto link; you review and send it yourself. If the draft is too long for a reliable link, the full text is shown for manual copying instead.

“Copy inquiry for webmail” writes the brief to your clipboard only when you choose that action. If clipboard access fails, the page displays selectable text. After you send an email, your mail provider and the receiving email systems handle it under their own arrangements. The website cannot confirm delivery or how long those systems retain a message.

Storage, hosting and other sites

Our website does not set cookies, use localStorage or sessionStorage, or load analytics, third-party fonts, embedded content or an external form service. It does not save your unfinished brief for a later visit. Your browser or mail application may offer its own autofill or draft features.

When you visit, the hosting provider may process access information needed to serve pages. We do not control the provider’s records. Technical articles link to outside references. The WhatsApp contact link opens an external service in a new tab; it does not send a message automatically. Those services are contacted only if you follow a link, and their own practices apply. This site asks the browser not to send the referring page address when navigating away.

Contact and changes

For questions about this site or a website inquiry, contact IngredientCore at ingredientcore@bespringchem.com. IngredientCore is a subsidiary of Bespring Chemical, established in 2025.
