Diammonium Phosphate DAP

Diammonium hydrogen phosphate for source-qualified nutrient and buffering discussions.

INGREDIENT OVERVIEW

DAP: product profile

Diammonium phosphate is diammonium hydrogen phosphate, an ammonium orthophosphate distinct from MAP. A source-specific specification is needed for the intended food or processing grade.

Also known as: DAP · Diammonium hydrogen phosphate · Ammonium phosphate dibasic

DAP is a distinct nutrient-system candidate from MAP. Its ammonium and phosphate contribution should be evaluated against the complete fermentation medium and the intended organism and process.

GRADE & PROCESS

Ammonium and phosphate balance in fermentation inputs

Choosing the material

A nitrogen-content target does not fully define DAP suitability. Identity, grade, composition basis and impurities remain separate requirements, and a similar nutrient claim on an agricultural product does not establish food-processing suitability.

Planning a useful comparison

Compare nutrient balance and pH behavior while holding the process conditions consistent. A qualified technical team should define the relevant process observations and finished-product checks.

PURCHASING QUESTIONS

DAP: what to ask next.

Does DAP always improve fermentation?

No. Evaluate the complete nutrient system, process and intended outcome. Nutrient identity does not establish the result for every organism or food.

What should accompany my inquiry?

Include the exact material and form, food application, destination market and target specification. Request current records for the proposed grade and source, and identify any methods your quality team requires.

Packaging, handling & ordering

Include the intended process and required material grade in the request. Ask for source-specific pack identification, storage and shelf-life records with the commercial terms.
