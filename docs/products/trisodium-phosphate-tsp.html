Trisodium Phosphate TSP

An alkaline sodium orthophosphate for application-specific pH and mineral-system evaluation.

INGREDIENT OVERVIEW

TSP: product profile

Trisodium phosphate is an alkaline sodium orthophosphate. Anhydrous and hydrated forms, including dodecahydrate, have different water content and formulation mass bases.

Also known as: TSP · Sodium phosphate tribasic · Tribasic sodium phosphate · Trisodium orthophosphate

TSP brings a more alkaline contribution than the less neutralized sodium orthophosphates. Selection therefore begins with the required food-system response and the complete acid, salt and mineral balance.

GRADE & PROCESS

Alkaline orthophosphate selection

Choosing the material

Anhydrous and hydrated TSP require separate material descriptions and calculations. Drying loss, assay expression and hydration identity must be read together; a water-related value is not interchangeable with phosphate content.

Planning a useful comparison

Observe pH through the process, not just in a stock solution. Compare taste, mineral precipitation and protein response where relevant, using the same water and addition sequence.

PURCHASING QUESTIONS

TSP: what to ask next.

Is TSP the same as STPP?

No. TSP is an orthophosphate and STPP is a tripolyphosphate. Compare identity, acid-base behavior and the intended function separately.

What should accompany my inquiry?

Include the exact material and form, food application, destination market and target specification. Request current records for the proposed grade and source, and identify any methods your quality team requires.
