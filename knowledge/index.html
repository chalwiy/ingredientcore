IngredientCore is a food ingredient export supplier and a subsidiary of Bespring Chemical. These eight guides help manufacturers, importers and distributors compare ingredient choices and prepare purchasing reviews.

Core products include food-grade phosphates, propionates, sorbates, Sodium CMC, xanthan gum and carrageenan. Citric acid and sodium citrate are included as selection context, rather than a core supply commitment.
