PRODUCT OVERVIEW

What is acidic SALP?

Acidic sodium aluminum phosphate is a leavening-acid material. The name SALP also needs the acidic type, composition and form; information for basic sodium aluminum phosphate is outside this bakery profile.

Also known as: SALP · Sodium aluminium phosphate, acidic · Acidic sodium aluminum phosphate

GRADE & COMPOSITION

Choose acidic SALP for your baking schedule

Resolve the exact acidic composition

Match the formula, hydration state and identity in the proposed specification, SDS and sample label. Declare any blend components separately. The term acidic SALP identifies a material family; it does not establish a particular commercial grade.

Separate neutralizing value from timing

Neutralizing value concerns acid capacity against sodium bicarbonate on a stated basis. Reaction tests concern gas release under defined mixing, holding and heating. Request both definitions and methods, not a generic fast/slow label.

Keep thermal loss and solution pH method-specific

Loss on ignition needs the temperature, duration and exact composition. It is not a moisture result or a loss-on-drying result. A reported pH also needs the preparation, solvent, concentration basis and temperature; it does not measure the grade’s neutralizing value.

A chemical assay, pH excerpt or ignition-loss value does not establish neutralizing value or bakery timing. Ask sales to identify the proposed acidic grade and records before comparing it with SAPP.

BUYER QUESTIONS

Acidic SALP purchasing questions

Is acidic SALP the same purchasing description as basic SALP?

No. This profile concerns an acidic bakery material. Require exact identity and composition; do not combine records for different SALP types.

Does a reaction code give the neutralizing value?

No. Capacity and gas-release timing are separate questions with separate methods. Confirm both for the sampled grade.

Are aluminum and aluminium different product names?

They are US and UK spellings of the same element. For procurement, the important distinctions are acidic versus basic type, exact composition, hydration state and the named grade.

Can I replace SAPP with SALP at the same weight?

No equivalence is assumed. Review exact acidic grade, acid capacity, bicarbonate balance and production timing with the formulator before a controlled comparison.
