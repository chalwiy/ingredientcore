Tetrasodium Pyrophosphate TSPP

An alkaline pyrophosphate considered for buffering, mineral interactions and emulsifying-salt systems.

INGREDIENT OVERVIEW

TSPP: product profile

Tetrasodium pyrophosphate is an alkaline diphosphate. Anhydrous and decahydrate forms require separate specifications; neither should be confused with sodium acid pyrophosphate.

Also known as: TSPP · Tetrasodium diphosphate · Sodium pyrophosphate · Sodium diphosphate tetrabasic

TSPP and SAPP are different materials even though both are pyrophosphates. In protein-food discussions, the alkaline contribution and interactions of TSPP should be evaluated within the complete salt and phosphate system.

PURCHASING QUESTIONS

TSPP: what to ask next.

Can TSPP replace SAPP in a leavening system?

Do not substitute on the pyrophosphate name. TSPP and SAPP have different acid-base roles; the complete acid–bicarbonate system needs its own design and trials.

What should accompany my inquiry?

Include the exact material and form, food application, destination market and target specification. Request current records for the proposed grade and source, and identify any methods your quality team requires.
