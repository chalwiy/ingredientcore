MATERIAL OVERVIEW

What is DATEM?

DATEM is short for diacetyltartaric acid esters of mono- and diglycerides, also written “diacetyl tartaric acid esters of mono- and diglycerides.” It describes a mixture of glycerol esters rather than a pure substance with one fixed molecular formula or molecular weight.

The FAO/JECFA identity description links its component distribution to raw materials and manufacturing conditions. A proposed grade therefore needs its own composition and source records; a generic identity description cannot establish its analytical limits or baking behavior.

Material versus formulated system

Ask whether the proposed item is a DATEM material or a formulated bakery improver containing DATEM. Identify any declared carriers or other components rather than assuming the whole system is DATEM.

Physical form & incorporation

Request the named grade’s actual form and handling guidance. Do not assume that a powder, granular product or another form is available from IngredientCore, or apply one source’s instructions to every grade.

Identity basis: FAO/JECFA Monographs 7 (2009), DATEM section. This reference describes a mixed ester material and the emulsifier functional class; it does not verify a commercial source or authorize every food use.

BAKERY EVALUATION

Plan a DATEM bakery comparison

For a yeast-raised bread review, define the dough-handling or baked-product question first. Treat volume, crumb, texture and sensory performance as separate trial observations. This guide does not promise improved tolerance, softness, volume or shelf life.

Flour & formulation

Name the flour system and current benchmark. Record water addition, fats and other improver components relevant to the comparison. Ask for the proposed grade’s incorporation guidance before planning a trial.

SELECTION QUESTIONS

DATEM selection questions

What does DATEM stand for?

Diacetyltartaric acid esters of mono- and diglycerides. It is a mixed ester material, not one pure molecule with a fixed formula or molecular weight.

Do matching acid values make two grades equivalent?

No equivalence follows from one field. Compare the complete source specifications, methods, composition statements, forms and relevant trial observations.

Does DATEM identify a plant-derived or certified material?

No. Request source-specific declarations and applicable certificate records. The material name alone establishes neither origin nor certification.

Can one DATEM grade be replaced by another at the same amount?

This guide provides no replacement ratio. Review both grades and their incorporation guidance, then define a controlled comparison in the actual food and process.

What should a purchasing-support request include?

State the food, current issue, intended grade or requirements, process conditions, destination and needed records. Sales first confirms support and source availability; no supply, sample or quotation is established by this page.
