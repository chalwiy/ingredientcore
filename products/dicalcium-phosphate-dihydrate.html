The dihydrate form of calcium hydrogen phosphate for form-specific mineral and processing requirements.

INGREDIENT OVERVIEW

DCP dihydrate: product profile

Dicalcium phosphate dihydrate is calcium hydrogen phosphate containing two waters of crystallization. It is a separate material identity from anhydrous DCP, not merely another particle grade.

Also known as: DCP dihydrate · Calcium hydrogen phosphate dihydrate · Dibasic calcium phosphate dihydrate · DCPD

Dicalcium phosphate dihydrate has its own hydration identity and composition basis. It belongs in a mineral-ingredient comparison as the dihydrate, rather than as an unspecified DCP substitute.

GRADE & PROCESS

Dihydrate DCP with a defined water basis

Choosing the material

The water associated with the dihydrate affects calculations and interpretation of heating tests. Separate hydration identity, assay basis and particle characteristics when reviewing a candidate grade.

Planning a useful comparison

Compare the intended food or premix using consistent dispersion and sampling. Record sediment, blend distribution or texture where relevant, and do not infer equal-mass equivalence to the anhydrous form.
