PRODUCT OVERVIEW

What is trisodium phosphate?

Trisodium phosphate is a tribasic sodium orthophosphate. Na₃PO₄ describes the anhydrous salt, while Na₃PO₄·12H₂O describes the dodecahydrate. Their material identity and mass-based analytical records must remain separate.

Also known as: TSP · Tribasic sodium phosphate · Sodium phosphate tribasic · Trisodium orthophosphate

Identity/background reference — material identity, not confirmation of an offered grade or destination-market approval.

BUYER QUESTIONS

TSP purchasing questions

Can a dried-basis assay be treated as the product’s water content?

No. The assay reports the named salt on a stated basis. Loss on drying is a separate test with its own procedure and form-dependent interpretation. Review both fields without subtracting one from the other to create an unreported specification.

Does the anhydrous table qualify dodecahydrate TSP?

No. Request a specification and identity tied to the hydrated form, with its own assay basis and water-related methods.

Is technical cleaning-grade TSP a food purchasing specification?

No. Request the food grade and source records appropriate to the intended food and destination. A shared material name is not source qualification.

Can solution pH alone determine food suitability?

No. Compare the complete recipe, preparation and process. A raw-material solution test does not predict every protein, mineral or sensory response.
