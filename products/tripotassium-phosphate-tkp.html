PRODUCT OVERVIEW

What is tripotassium phosphate?

Tripotassium phosphate is a tribasic potassium orthophosphate. JECFA lists buffer, emulsion-stabilizing and sequestrant functions for the material. Selecting TKP for a particular food requires a process trial; the listed functions do not establish the performance of a proposed supply. TKP and tetrapotassium pyrophosphate (TKPP) are different substances.

Also known as: TKP · Tribasic potassium phosphate · Potassium phosphate tribasic · Tripotassium orthophosphate

Identity/background reference — material identity, not confirmation of an offered grade or destination-market approval.

GRADE & PREPARATION

TKP form and preparation requirements

Hydration state

Match the product identity on the proposed specification, SDS, sample label and COA. Anhydrous or hydrated identity describes the material; dry or ignited basis describes how an analytical result is calculated. One does not establish the other.

Alkaline preparation

Describe the food, acids and other phosphate salts, target pH, mixing sequence and process temperature. Use the source instructions and current SDS when preparing the proposed material. Assess pH in the complete food at defined stages, separately from a laboratory solution result.

BUYER QUESTIONS

TKP purchasing questions

Are TKP and TKPP interchangeable names?

No. TKP identifies tripotassium orthophosphate; TKPP identifies tetrapotassium pyrophosphate, a condensed phosphate with a different formula.

Is loss on ignition the same as water content?

No. A thermal-loss result depends on the material form, temperature, duration and method. Ask which test and reporting basis apply; do not use a loss-on-ignition value as a moisture result without that definition.

Does potassium content establish a low-sodium food claim?

No. A formulation goal is separate from the finished-food composition and the applicable destination-market labeling criteria. Evaluate the full formulation and the intended claim separately.

Can I replace a sodium phosphate at the same weight?

No general equal-weight substitution is established. Compare phosphate identity, material form, reporting basis and behavior in the complete food process.

How do I request a sample or quotation?

Share the product, food application, target specification, quantity and destination. Free sample arrangements are discussed with sales; request the documents needed for your evaluation.
