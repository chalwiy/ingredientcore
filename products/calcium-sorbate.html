Calcium Sorbate CaS

A sorbate option whose suitability depends on solubility, process conditions and the food category.

INGREDIENT OVERVIEW

What this ingredient
can help you explore.

Calcium sorbate is a calcium salt of sorbic acid. Its exact food-grade identity, form and permitted use need separate assessment.

Also known as: CaS · Calcium salt of sorbic acid

Calcium sorbate is less commonly selected than potassium sorbate in many liquid systems. Its dispersion and calcium contribution should be checked before considering substitution.

PURCHASING QUESTIONS

CaS: what to ask next.

Can potassium sorbate results be carried over?

No. Compare incorporation, calcium contribution and finished-food results under the same conditions, and check the particular market and food category.

Which records should I request for source qualification?

Request a current specification and relevant batch or sample records for the proposed grade and source. Include your destination market and any additional qualification requirements.

Packaging, handling & ordering

Include volume, delivery destination and required timing in the inquiry. Ask about available pack formats, minimum order, sample terms and transport arrangements for the specific offer. Confirm storage instructions, shelf life and label details against the proposed source’s current records.
