PRODUCT OVERVIEW

What is calcium sorbate?

Calcium sorbate is the calcium salt of sorbic acid. The WHO/JECFA identity record lists CAS 7492-55-9. An identity listing does not establish permission in a particular food or market, or the specification of a proposed supply.

Also known as: Calcium salt of sorbic acid

JECFA identity reference — material identity, not confirmation of an offered grade or destination-market approval.

BUYER QUESTIONS

Calcium sorbate purchasing questions

Does a chemical catalog listing establish a food grade?

No. Request a food-use specification and qualification records for the proposed source, together with the destination requirements. A research material listing, CAS number or certificate title alone does not qualify a product for your food.

What should the assay record identify?

Ask for the reported substance, units, dry or as-received basis, method and acceptance limits. Keep these together when comparing the specification and COA; do not assume that a sorbic acid value is a calcium sorbate value.

Can I use potassium sorbate specifications for calcium sorbate?

No. Obtain records for the calcium material actually proposed, including its own identity, form, limits and methods.

What should I provide before requesting a sample?

State the food category, destination, intended process and reason for considering the calcium salt. Sales can discuss the proposed source, grade, free sample and shipping arrangements.

Does a JECFA identity entry mean it is permitted everywhere?

No. Material identification and destination-market use requirements are separate. Review the current rules for the particular food before planning use.
