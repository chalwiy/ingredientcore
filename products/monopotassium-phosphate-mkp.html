PRODUCT OVERVIEW

What is monopotassium phosphate?

Monopotassium phosphate is potassium dihydrogen phosphate, a monobasic potassium orthophosphate. Its identity differs from dibasic DKP, tribasic TKP and condensed potassium phosphates.

Also known as: MKP · Potassium dihydrogen phosphate · Monobasic potassium phosphate · Potassium phosphate monobasic

Identity/background reference — material identity, not confirmation of an offered grade or destination-market approval.

BUYER QUESTIONS

MKP purchasing questions

Is potassium phosphate a precise name for MKP?

It is a family description. Use monopotassium phosphate or potassium dihydrogen phosphate, together with MKP and the proposed material specification.

Does the published assay describe every offered grade?

No. It is a field from supplied product information on a dry basis. The signed specification and COA for the proposed source must establish the purchasing criteria.

Is loss on ignition the same as moisture?

No. Thermal-loss fields need their own test definition, temperature and duration. Ask for the named method; do not use an ignition-loss value as the moisture content or the drying procedure for the assay.

Can I replace a sodium phosphate at the same weight?

No general equal-weight substitution is established. Compare phosphate identity, material form, reporting basis and behavior in the complete food process.

How do I request a sample or quotation?

Share the product, food application, target specification, quantity and destination. Free sample arrangements are discussed with sales; request the documents needed for your evaluation.
