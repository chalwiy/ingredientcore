Dicalcium Phosphate Anhydrous DCP anhydrous

INGREDIENT OVERVIEW

DCP anhydrous: product profile

Dicalcium phosphate anhydrous is calcium hydrogen phosphate without water of crystallization. It requires different form-specific records from dicalcium phosphate dihydrate.

Also known as: DCP anhydrous · DCP · Calcium hydrogen phosphate anhydrous · Dibasic calcium phosphate anhydrous

DCP anhydrous can enter discussions about calcium-phosphate composition and particulate behavior in foods or premixes. The required function should guide the specification: mineral composition and dispersion are different purchasing questions.

GRADE & PROCESS

Anhydrous calcium hydrogen phosphate

Choosing the material

Anhydrous DCP and DCP dihydrate differ in hydration and mass basis. An anhydrous-basis assay does not, by itself, prove that a delivered material is physically anhydrous.

Planning a useful comparison

Compare particle behavior, dispersion and visible sediment in the intended system. Keep water chemistry and processing consistent, and use the relevant composition basis for any formulation calculation.
