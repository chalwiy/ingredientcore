PRODUCT OVERVIEW

What is sodium trimetaphosphate?

STMP is a cyclic condensed phosphate, with the supplied formula Na₃(PO₃)₃, also written Na₃P₃O₉. It is distinct from sodium orthophosphates, STPP and commercial SHMP; their names or phosphate percentages do not make them interchangeable.

Also known as: STMP · Trisodium trimetaphosphate · Cyclic sodium trimetaphosphate

JECFA modified-starches reference — finished-starch specification context, not an STMP reagent specification.

BUYER QUESTIONS

STMP purchasing questions

Is STMP another trade name for SHMP?

No. STMP is the named cyclic trimetaphosphate; commercial SHMP is a glassy polyphosphate material. Compare exact identities and intended processes.

Does P₂O₅ content express STMP compound assay?

No. Identify the analyte, basis and method of each field. A phosphate-equivalent report and compound assay should not be treated as the same percentage.

What needs to accompany a reported pH?

The reference excerpt states a 1% solution. A comparison also needs the solvent, concentration basis, temperature and method. A pH result does not establish starch modification or finished-food performance.

Does an identity listing authorize direct use in every food?

No. Identity, reagent qualification and food/process permission are separate. A food-contact substance record is not universal direct-food authorization.
