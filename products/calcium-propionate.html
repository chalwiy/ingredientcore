Calcium Propionate CaP

A propionate commonly screened to support mold control in bakery products.

INGREDIENT OVERVIEW

What this ingredient
can help you explore.

Calcium propionate is a calcium salt of propionic acid. Its food-grade identity, assay basis and physical form need to be tied to the proposed source.

Also known as: CaP · Calcium propanoate

Performance depends on formulation pH, water activity, process hygiene, packaging and storage. Calcium contribution may also matter in the recipe.

Evaluate the complete bakery preservation system

Describe the system: Describe the bakery food, pH, yeast/leavening system, process, packaging, storage conditions and target shelf life.

Observe in a controlled trial: Have qualified personnel plan microbiological evaluation through intended storage. Assess dough behavior and sensory outcome on appropriate samples. Hygiene, packaging and necessary safety controls remain essential; no universal dose or shelf-life extension follows from the name.

A trial informs selection for that material and process; it is not a guarantee for a different grade or future batch.

PURCHASING QUESTIONS

CaP: what to ask next.

How should I describe the needed grade?

Specify food-grade identity, preferred form, assay basis and relevant process requirements. Give the application and market so the source and records can be discussed for that use.

Is a COA evidence of a shelf-life claim?

A COA reports results for its identified sample or batch. It does not demonstrate the shelf life of a finished bread, tortilla or other food. Evaluate the complete food and package.

What changes when comparing sodium propionate?

Compare cation contribution, dissolution, taste and preservation behavior in the same recipe. Do not assume equal-weight substitution or identical process effects.

Packaging, handling & ordering

Include volume, delivery destination and required timing in the inquiry. Ask about available pack formats, minimum order, sample terms and transport arrangements for the specific offer. Confirm storage instructions, shelf life and label details against the proposed source’s current records.

DOCUMENT REQUEST

Request documents for CaP.
