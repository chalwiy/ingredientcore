PRODUCT OVERVIEW

What is diammonium phosphate?

Diammonium Phosphate is diammonium hydrogen phosphate, an ammonium orthophosphate. Its identity formula is (NH₄)₂HPO₄, distinct from monoammonium phosphate (MAP). The linked JECFA monograph lists yeast food, acidity-regulating and dough-conditioning functions. These general functions do not establish suitability or performance in every food process.

Also known as: DAP · Diammonium hydrogen phosphate · Ammonium phosphate dibasic

FAO/JECFA: diammonium hydrogen phosphate — material identity, not confirmation of an offered grade or destination-market approval.

Request the proposed source specification, SDS and impurity methods for the intended food use. Identify the issuer, revision and product designation across the specification, SDS and sample records. Fertilizer nitrogen/phosphate figures alone do not qualify this food purchase.

Nutrient reporting and incorporation

Ask whether an assay is expressed as (NH₄)₂HPO₄, nitrogen as N, phosphorus as P or a P₂O₅ equivalent. Keep the units, calculation and dry/as-is basis with each result. Also request the actual physical form, declared additions and preparation information for the proposed grade; handling form is a separate question from nutrient contribution.

BUYER QUESTIONS

DAP purchasing questions

Is DAP the same material as MAP?

No. DAP is diammonium hydrogen phosphate, (NH₄)₂HPO₄; MAP is ammonium dihydrogen phosphate. Compare their identities and analytical records against your process objective before considering a change.

Can a nitrogen percentage be used as the DAP assay?

No. Nitrogen content, phosphate expressions and DAP compound assay report different quantities. Request the analyte, units, basis and method for each field.

What is needed for a fermentation sample request?

Name the food and organism, the current nutrient inputs, addition stage and pH-control approach. Include the evaluation target and destination market. The sample should have an identified grade, source and relevant COA so your trial can be linked to the proposed order.

Does the yeast-food function establish permission for my food?

A general function or identity record does not establish permission for every food and destination. Your responsible team should assess the specific use and labeling requirements separately from the material specification and application trial.

Which records can I request before a DAP quotation?

Request the current specification and methods, TDS, COA, SDS and customer-required certificates. Send the target grade, food application, estimated quantity and destination. Free sample quantity and shipping arrangements are discussed with sales.
