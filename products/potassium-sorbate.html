PRODUCT OVERVIEW

What is potassium sorbate?

Potassium sorbate is the potassium salt of sorbic acid. Its material identity, chemical assay and physical form must be qualified separately from the finished food’s preservation objective.

Also known as: Potassium salt of sorbic acid

JECFA identity reference — material identity, not confirmation of an offered grade or destination-market approval.

FORM & PROCESS

Incorporating granular potassium sorbate

Specify form and incorporation

The supplied information describes granules. If your process requires a different form, request a corresponding proposal and specification rather than assuming the same physical profile.

Evaluate the actual food conditions

Describe the food, pH, water activity if measured, heat and filling steps, distribution method and storage. Chemical purity alone does not establish microbial performance.

Record the preparation and distribution step

For a granular sample, record whether it is added directly or prepared before addition, the liquid used where applicable, preparation temperature, mixing time and addition sequence. Observe visible undissolved material and distribution in the finished food alongside the agreed quality and preservation checks. Keep these observations linked to the sample grade and lot.

BUYER QUESTIONS

Potassium sorbate purchasing questions

Does a material heat test qualify my food process?

No. A material test has its own temperature, duration and endpoint. Request the method and evaluate the proposed ingredient in your actual heating, filling and storage process; a material result does not demonstrate finished-food stability.

Does the assay report sorbic acid content?

The selected field reports potassium sorbate content on a dry basis. It is not a sorbic acid content value or a recipe conversion factor. Compare results using the stated material and assay method.

Does the drying test define the assay method?

No. The attachment states dry-basis assay and a separate drying test at 105°C for 3 hours. Request the assay method and basis calculation for the proposed source.

Can a calcium sorbate result qualify potassium sorbate?

No. Identify the exact salt and grade, review its own records and incorporation behavior, and evaluate it in the defined food and market.

Does a broth inhibition result establish shelf life?

No. Laboratory media, organisms and test conditions differ from the final food, package and storage system. Use a food-specific professional evaluation.
