Xanthan Gum Xanthan

A hydrocolloid commonly evaluated for viscosity, suspension and flow control.

INGREDIENT OVERVIEW

What this ingredient
can help you explore.

Xanthan gum is a hydrocolloid used as a candidate for viscosity and suspension. A commercial mesh or viscosity designation must be read with the producer’s grade definition.

Also known as: Xanthan · Xanthan

Xanthan can give strong low-shear viscosity and shear-thinning flow. Dispersion and hydration method affect lumping and final texture.

PURCHASING QUESTIONS

Xanthan: what to ask next.

Is mesh size a complete grade description?

No. Include viscosity conditions, identity and other acceptance fields. Mesh describes a particle test and should be linked to its method and the actual grade.

Why request the salt conditions of the viscosity test?

The composition and preparation of the test solution affect the comparison. Obtain the complete method instead of assuming that all reported viscosity figures are measured alike.

What should a suspension trial observe?

Check settling at rest, ease of pouring, mixing, mouthfeel and changes after heat and storage. Compare with a control under the same process and target conditions.

Packaging, handling & ordering

Include volume, delivery destination and required timing in the inquiry. Ask about available pack formats, minimum order, sample terms and transport arrangements for the specific offer. Confirm storage instructions, shelf life and label details against the proposed source’s current records.
