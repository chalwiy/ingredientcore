PRODUCT OVERVIEW

What is xanthan gum?

Xanthan gum is a fermentation-derived polysaccharide used in food thickening and stabilization. A particle-size description and a solution-viscosity result address different purchasing questions.

Buyers consider xanthan for sauces and dressings, particle-containing beverages and dry mixes. Assess powder incorporation and hydration alongside the flow and suspension requirements of the finished food.

PARTICLES & HYDRATION

Particle size, dispersion and hydration

Ask for a defined particle profile

A mesh designation describes a particle-size requirement, not the full grade. Request the sieve standard, passing percentage or distribution, and the exact grade to which the result applies. Relate the powder profile to your addition and mixing route.

Separate dispersion from hydration

Dispersion distributes the powder through the liquid; hydration is the subsequent interaction with water. Record whether powder is dry-blended or added to liquid, the addition sequence, water and salts, mixing, temperature and time. Compare viscosity only after the defined preparation is complete.

Define the observations before the trial

  • At rest: record the observation interval, storage temperature, sediment or separation, and how the food is redispersed.
  • During use: compare pouring, coating and mouthfeel at a stated serving temperature and using the same handling procedure.
  • After processing: record heat and mixing history, elapsed time before measurement, and the sample grade and lot. Keep these observations separate from the supplier’s solution-viscosity test.

These are evaluation questions. Review the exact food category, use conditions and labeling for the destination market separately from sample performance.

BUYER QUESTIONS

Xanthan gum purchasing questions

Does a solution-viscosity result predict finished-food flow?

It is a controlled comparison of the material under the reported test conditions. The food trial has its own recipe, preparation and observations. Keep both records so purchasing can compare the proposed grade while the technical team evaluates its use.

Does finer mesh automatically mean a better grade?

No. Particle profile, incorporation route and measurement method must match the intended process. Judge the named sample in the food, rather than ranking by a mesh heading alone.

What should a suspension trial record?

Record particles, formulation, preparation and storage; observe settling, redispersibility, pouring and mouthfeel at defined stages. A single solution-viscosity figure does not answer every observation.

Can I compare two reported shear values?

Only after obtaining the definition, units and method for each. An unspecified index is not a directly comparable flow measurement.
