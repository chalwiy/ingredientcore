Sodium Citrate SC

A citrate salt evaluated for buffering, mineral management and flavor balance.

INGREDIENT OVERVIEW

What this ingredient
can help you explore.

Sodium citrate is a naming starting point. Specify the sodium citrate salt and hydrate form; a trisodium citrate inquiry should not be treated as an unspecified citrate mixture.

Also known as: SC · Trisodium citrate · Citrate salts

Sodium citrate can modify pH behavior and interact with minerals. The hydrate form and sodium contribution should be considered in formula calculations.

PURCHASING QUESTIONS

SC: what to ask next.

What should accompany the name sodium citrate?

Include the intended salt, hydrate, grade and source. Ask for a specification that uses the same identity.

How should it be compared with citric acid?

State whether the objective is pH reduction, buffering or mineral interaction, then compare the complete acid/salt system and sensory results. This page is a technical guide, not a supply offer.

Packaging, handling & ordering

Include volume, delivery destination and required timing in the inquiry. Ask about available pack formats, minimum order, sample terms and transport arrangements for the specific offer. Confirm storage instructions, shelf life and label details against the proposed source’s current records.

DOCUMENT REQUEST

Request documents for SC.

State the grade, source if known, food application, destination market and purpose. Ask about Exact-salt/hydrate specification, representative COA and relevant TDS, if a source is proposed; confirm availability. Availability and permission to share must be confirmed for the proposed supply. Certificates must match their own entity, site and scope.
