PRODUCT OVERVIEW

What is MCP monohydrate?

Monocalcium phosphate monohydrate is calcium dihydrogen phosphate with one water of crystallization. The monohydrate is distinct from anhydrous MCP. For procurement, request source records explicitly naming the monohydrate and identifying the sample; an unspecified MCP sheet does not establish the supplied form.

Also known as: MCP monohydrate · Calcium dihydrogen phosphate monohydrate · Monobasic calcium phosphate monohydrate

Identity/background reference — material identity, not confirmation of an offered grade or destination-market approval.

GRADE & FORM

Choose the MCP monohydrate baking grade

Keep the sample traceable

Ask for the grade name, producer/source and sample or lot identifier. Check that the specification, TDS, COA and SDS refer to the same monohydrate material. Request particle information for the incorporation route rather than assuming every product called MCP is the same powder or granule.

Three measurements, three questions

Calcium analysis describes a measured component; neutralizing value describes acid-balance capacity under a defined method; a reaction test describes behavior over time under its test conditions. Keep these records separate when reviewing a commercial grade. None is a substitute for a trial of your complete bicarbonate and acid system.

BUYER QUESTIONS

MCP monohydrate purchasing questions

Does MCP alone identify the monohydrate?

No. MCP is also used without a hydration qualifier. This page reviews the monohydrate identity; request matching form-specific records for the actual sample and proposed order.

Can calcium content predict a baking result?

No. Calcium analysis, neutralizing value and reaction data answer different questions. Compare the grade in the complete formulation and process, with a defined mixing and heating history.

Does drying loss measure all water in the monohydrate?

A thermal-loss result is method-dependent. Ask for the stated test conditions and distinguish crystallization water, free moisture and other mass changes rather than treating every drying or ignition result as the same measurement.

What makes a sample request useful?

Name MCP monohydrate, the food, current leavening system, mix-to-cook interval and observations to compare. Request the specification, grade TDS, sample or representative COA and SDS. Free samples and customer-required certificates can be discussed with sales for the proposed product and source.
