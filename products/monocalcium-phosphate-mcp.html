Monocalcium Phosphate Monohydrate MCP monohydrate

The monohydrate form of calcium dihydrogen phosphate for leavening-system comparisons.

INGREDIENT OVERVIEW

MCP monohydrate: product profile

Monocalcium phosphate monohydrate is the hydrated form of calcium dihydrogen phosphate. It has a distinct formulation mass basis from the anhydrous material and needs a form-specific specification.

Also known as: MCP monohydrate · MCP · Calcium dihydrogen phosphate monohydrate · Monobasic calcium phosphate monohydrate

Monocalcium phosphate monohydrate is an acid-phosphate direction for chemical leavening. Selection connects the acid component to bicarbonate, mixing, holding and heating, rather than relying on the MCP abbreviation alone.

GRADE & PROCESS

Monohydrate MCP in leavening selection

Choosing the material

Monohydrate and anhydrous MCP are separate materials. The water incorporated in the material and its commercial reaction characteristics both matter to a recipe comparison; calcium content alone is not a reaction-rate specification.

Planning a useful comparison

Compare acid–bicarbonate balance and the actual mix-to-bake interval. Observe batter or dough pH, rise, crumb and taste with the same ingredients and heating schedule.

PURCHASING QUESTIONS

MCP monohydrate: what to ask next.

Can an anhydrous MCP specification cover this form?

No. Ask for records naming the monohydrate. Compare recipe calculations and commercial reaction behavior before considering a change of form.

What should accompany my inquiry?

Include the exact material and form, food application, destination market and target specification. Request current records for the proposed grade and source, and identify any methods your quality team requires.

Packaging, handling & ordering

Name the monohydrate explicitly when requesting a sample or quotation. Include dry-mix handling conditions and ask for the corresponding pack and source storage instructions.
