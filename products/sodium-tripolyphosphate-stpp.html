PRODUCT OVERVIEW

What is sodium tripolyphosphate?

STPP is a sodium phosphate also known as pentasodium triphosphate. It is used in selected food systems for moisture and texture management; the exact grade and formulation determine how it fits your process.

Common names: Sodium Tripolyphosphate (STPP), Pentasodium Triphosphate, Pentasodium Tripolyphosphate and Sodium Triphosphate.

For purchasing, specify food-grade STPP, the required form and standard, your food application and destination market. Discuss the proposed source and supporting records with sales.

Identity reference: FAO/JECFA — Pentasodium Triphosphate. The reference identifies the substance; it does not certify an IngredientCore product or establish permission for every food and market.

BUYER QUESTIONS

STPP purchasing questions

Is P₂O₅ content the same as STPP content?

No. STPP content is reported as Na₅P₃O₁₀; the separate P₂O₅ value uses a different expression. Ask for the analytical method and calculation basis for each field rather than treating the figures as interchangeable.

Do these values describe every food-grade STPP product?

No. The table is a selected extract from supplied product information. The exact grade, source, analytical basis and complete acceptance criteria must be identified in the specification agreed for your purchase.

What should I specify when choosing powder or granules?

State whether the ingredient enters a dry blend or a prepared solution or brine. Include particle-size requirements where relevant, preparation temperature, mixing sequence and dissolution time, together with your food application.

Can STPP replace SHMP at the same addition level?

An equal-weight replacement cannot be assumed from the product names. Review the grade and intended function, then compare the candidates in the complete food and process. The STPP versus SHMP guide helps structure that discussion.

Can I request a free sample and certificates?

Yes. Contact sales about a free STPP sample and the certificates needed for your purchasing review. Sales will confirm the sample arrangements and which product, source, certificate holder and scope the records cover.

What information helps you prepare an STPP quotation?

Include the required food grade or standard, powder or granular preference, food application, target specification, estimated quantity, destination market and required documents. Sales can then discuss the proposed material and commercial terms.
