BUYER QUESTIONS

A few answers before you contact us.

What food ingredients does IngredientCore supply?

Our core portfolio includes food-grade phosphates, calcium and sodium propionate, potassium and calcium sorbate, sodium carboxymethyl cellulose (CMC), xanthan gum, carrageenan and related hydrocolloids. Browse our product directory.

Which export markets do you serve?

We serve food manufacturers, ingredient importers and distributors worldwide. Include the destination country and delivery destination in your inquiry so we can discuss the relevant product requirements and export terms.

Can I request a free sample?

Yes. Free samples are available; please contact our sales team for details. Tell us the ingredient, required grade and application so we can discuss your sample request. Request a free sample.

Can you provide the certificates we need?

Yes. We can provide customer-required certificates. Send the product, destination market and certificate requirements to sales to confirm the applicable holder, product, source and scope. Request certificates.

What should I include in a quotation request?

Include the product name, grade or target specification, food application, required quantity, destination and timing. Add any sample or document requirements. Prepare a quotation inquiry.

Is IngredientCore a manufacturer or an export company?

IngredientCore is a food ingredient export company established in 2025 and a subsidiary of Bespring Chemical. The producer and manufacturing site are identified for the specific product offer. Read our company introduction.
