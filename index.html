Purchasing questions

What should I include in a quote request?

Include the ingredient, estimated quantity and destination. Add the grade, food application and requested timing if known. Prepare a quote request.

Can I request a free sample?

Yes. Tell sales which product you need and what you plan to evaluate. Sales will discuss sample quantity and shipping with you. Request a free sample.

How can I review certificates for my product?

Tell sales the product, destination and documents you require. For the proposed source, check the certificate holder, site, scope and validity. See the document guide.

Can I ask about an ingredient before choosing a grade?

Yes. Describe your food, the processing conditions and the function you need. Discuss your application with sales.
